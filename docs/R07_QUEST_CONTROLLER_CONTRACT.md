## Minimal coordination layer

Reuse Core Goals, workflow definitions/runs, worker definitions, immutable installation snapshots and artifacts. A controller-created child objective is an immutable scoped overlay, not another financial Goal. Its workflow retains the exact parent `goal_id`; its Business remains the R05 authority root. Children inherit the parent's policy, purpose/operation, currency and expiry, with equal or narrower aggregate caps. Each step cap includes all its repair attempts; R05 still enforces the smaller per-operation quote/cap independently. No child, repair or pivot creates a confirmation, policy, new allowance or external account.

Plans use `r07.1`, exact R04 objective/rules revision and content hashes, exact confirmed R05 policy ID/hash, and a finite topologically ordered list of at most 16 steps. Each step pins its adapter qualification, installation snapshot, workflow and worker definition/role, purpose/operation, artifact type, dependencies, budget, expiry, earliest start, optional measurement window, objective and reason. The first step is research, the second is independent challenge, and every later step directly depends on that challenge. Check workers differ from the planner and the workers whose output they check. Required checks and explicit finish/stop rules are stored. No unrestricted model loop exists.

The deterministic compiler performs no network/model action. SQL independently validates the contract; client validation is not authority. Qualification, Knowledge freshness, pack snapshot, current owner, R04 pins, R05 policy proof/confirmation/revocation and scoped pause are rechecked before reservation and immediately before dispatch. Qualified adapter definitions are an administrator-only immutable registry. The migration seeds neither adapters nor controller keys. The production adapter map is empty; inert fixtures supply explicit test adapters. A saved plan or installed migration cannot turn on production execution.

## Persistence, locking and authority

New tables are private with RLS, no direct anon/authenticated/service-role access, explicit function ACLs and empty search paths. Immutable plans, child scopes, registry evidence, dispatch markers, responses, reuse links and events cannot be rewritten. A small mutable head and attempt-state projection is updated only by guarded RPCs. The authenticated owner read is bounded, nonlocking and pure; exact Business/Quest/plan selection is owner-verified independently of directory pages.

The trusted controller RPC needs its own separately provisioned expiring/revocable server credential. R05 reservation, marking and settlement additionally need the existing R05 server authority. Neither key enters saved payloads, workflow input, worker context, events, errors or owner reads. New persistent access is deliberately unconfigured and requires separate deployment review/approval.

All mutations lock the Business before the Quest head, matching R04/R05's financial/pause ordering. A lease has an opaque owner token hash, monotonically increasing epoch and bounded wall-clock expiry. New claims fence old controllers. Scheduling, reserve, dispatch, finish, cancel and plan changes require the exact live lease and current plan revision. Immutable callback evidence may be ingested after lease expiry/pause by trusted receipt ingestion; it cannot launch work or advance unrelated steps. The next current controller projects that evidence. Submission replay verifies exact payload identity; replaying a dispatch receipt always returns `shouldDispatch=false`.

## Reserve → dispatch → response → persist

1. Schedule one ready step durably, creating exactly one Core workflow and child scope before adapter work. Persist its stable attempt ID. A controller derives the per-run capability from its server secret and that ID without persisting plaintext credentials. Adapter preparation is side-effect-free and produces the exact bounded R05 wire descriptor.
2. Reserve through the existing R05 RPC and save the exact request binding. Require the selected request's policy, workflow, wire hash, operation, account and liability to match the plan; R05 chooses and verifies authority independently. A newer different policy cannot silently replace a plan pin.
3. Atomically create the R05 dispatch marker and R07 attempt marker after fresh checks. Only the first successful response permits a send. A crash or lost response after marking leaves an explicit uncertain attempt; never resend it. Reconciliation is read-only and needs exact trusted readback; absence of a result is not proof no effect occurred.
4. Persist trusted result/receipt and R05 settlement atomically. An unknown actual charge stays unknown. Exact duplicate responses are no-ops; conflicting responses, receipt reuse and cross-attempt/cross-Business callbacks fail. The response snapshot and output artifact are immutable. A stale controller cannot turn a rejected output into acceptance.
5. Project the persisted result under a current lease. Completion means required outputs/checks verified, never realised profit or target achievement. Business state does not change automatically. Rejected checks retain their outputs and stop dependent work. In-flight settlement/reconciliation stays available after pause, expiry or owner cancellation.

Process-local state is disposable. Fresh processes reconstruct exclusively from persisted plan/attempt/result snapshots. Successful steps are not repeated. A pivot may reuse an exact unchanged successful step through an explicit immutable reuse link; challenge/review results are never reused against a new plan hash. Superseded plans and unsuccessful attempts remain inspectable.

## Finite repair, pivot and wait

Hard maxima are eight repairs, three pivots, 32 child scopes and 64 dispatches across the complete Quest controller lineage, with tighter plan/policy bounds retained from the initial plan. Repairs need the verified hash of the prior immutable rejected/failed result body, a changed fingerprint and recorded reason, that rejected artifact included in the new input pins, a terminal settled prior attempt, remaining per-step/global allowance and full fresh admission. Pivots require existing rejected/failed-result evidence and preserve the same objective/rules/policy/root and cannot widen budgets, expiry, bounds, accounts or purpose. New scope requires an owner exception and new reviewed authority; technical missing capability stays Blocked.

Measurement uses persisted earliest-start/close times and minimum observations. Waiting schedules a timestamp or awaits changed external evidence; it never calls a model to pass time. Insufficient evidence is not success. Expired or exhausted work stops. A plan stops when no useful permitted work remains, retaining partial results and unresolved liability rather than resetting either.
