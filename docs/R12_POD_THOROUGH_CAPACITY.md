## Quality failure and financial lifecycle

- Honest zero-result collection and zero selected quotes reach an independent review. Empty evidence must yield INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE with failed source/claim checks, not vacuous all-pass validation.
- An insufficient reviewer supplies critique for a new planner, which retains prior queries and evidence history and chooses a different search inside the approved policy.
- Only explicit attributable formatting/capacity failures are eligible for counted continuation. Exact dispatched identity, immutable observation/diagnostic, known bounded amount and verified generation route are required. Unknown liability, unsafe source URLs, wrong identity/route, missing receipts or authority failures do not qualify.
- The failed pass remains incomplete, has no accepted review and consumes its real calls/search/pass allowance. Accepted earlier outputs and labelled failure history are untrusted context, not accepted review dependencies. The next pass requires a fresh ordinary independent review.
- Successful and failed effects share a maximum of3 receipt checks, scheduled at real saved wake times. Metadata retries cannot create generations. Stop prevents new dispatch while exact existing receipts can reconcile within their cutoff.
- Normal, fully awaited step completion releases only the exact owned lease. Abort, unresolved transport or stale ownership cannot release a different worker's fence. A fresh step otherwise waits for the existing lease.
- Temporary competing unissued or exactly verified unmarked reservations can return a bounded funding wait rather than permanently completing the Goal. The full held-cost inequality is rechecked on every wake; settled costs, marked/unknown effects and the old Steel hold are not waived.
- A paid transition starts by absolute20s in the270s runtime request, leaving headroom for the conservative225s operation chain. Each new call remains atomically reserved under existing financial gates.
