## Authority and history

The new `r12.discovery-episode.1` plan format is usable only with database-verified
closure and activation evidence. Increasing a version number, changing Business
wording, or creating a duplicate Goal does not supply that evidence. The older
plan formats retain their existing limits.

Closure is reconstructed from persisted history. A historical `running` label
does not imply executable authority, and is not rewritten to fabricate completion.
The predecessor must have no unresolved dispatch, pending or unknown liability,
or executable policy/capability. Confirmation checks the same closure again under
the existing financial and controller locks. A unique predecessor linkage prevents
two successors; a repeated identical confirmation returns the saved activation.

Two private immutable ledgers retain predecessor closure and episode activation.
The finite grant explicitly bounds episode count, cumulative allocation and expiry.
Existing grants acquire no continuation permission automatically. Stop consumes an
activated episode even when it has sent no call. It cannot replenish an allocation
or reset the 32-child and 64-dispatch lifetime ceilings.

The Goal's cumulative counters and known costs continue across episodes. Each new
phase budget is episode-local. Business exposure and the original funding root
remain cumulative overlapping accounting views, never two amounts to add together.
Native R05 Business funding and legacy research-root funding keep their identities.

independent review. No two-call substitute, evidence-addendum restart or fabricated
phase is introduced. Inert test outcomes qualify software behavior only.

The StudioKindredStore geographic Quest remains canonical. A GB-only objective has
not been confirmed. The earlier accepted NEEDS_MORE_EVIDENCE result remains history;

new evidence can answer and which remain unknown. TEST is permitted only when the
actual evidence supports its bounded private-learning meaning; it is not forced.

The reviewed profile pins a public prompt and domain filters. It does not promise

inference before selection. Inference retention controls do not establish Exa
retention or residency. Source permission, recipients, model routes and prices must
