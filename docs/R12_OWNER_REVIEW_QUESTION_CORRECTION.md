It does not truncate responses, increase schema limits, discard inherited questions or accept the failed historical response.

The regression uses three candidates and 27 inherited questions, verifies that a review with no additional questions preserves all 27, and verifies that more than 18 additional questions still fail. Technical tests cannot prove how a future model response will behave.
