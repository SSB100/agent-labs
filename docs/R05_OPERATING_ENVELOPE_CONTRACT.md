# R05 operating envelope and admission contract

Status: implemented local R05 boundary, baseline `7533195`, 2026-10-03; independent final security review and actual PostgreSQL multi-session/CI gates remain pending. No provider activation, paid call or new live authority follows from this code. R06–R18 remain separate work (R19 remains in the master plan).

## Authority separation

R04 confirmations remain intent-only. They contain arbitrary operation text and lack cumulative/per-operation limits and executable stopping semantics. R05 adds a separately versioned **structured operating policy** with its own exact owner confirmation, bound to immutable R04 Business/Quest revisions and content hashes. It does not reinterpret old confirmations or replace existing exact approvals. Confirming the complete new policy is the initial operational consent; a worker cannot sign it, increase it or substitute a newer version.

The policy declares currency, a cumulative Business lifetime ceiling, a ceiling for this policy/Quest, category ceilings, per-operation ceilings, finite operations and exact pack/workflow/purpose/provider/account/source/data-use bindings, validity interval, maximum dispatch count, minimum interval between calls, and supported stop conditions. Decimal input uses strings; storage uses integer millionths of the stated currency. Different currencies never combine. FX/conversion, borrowing against revenue, negative/refund offsets and financial-profit claims are unsupported. Revenue, marketplace fees, supplier charges and refunds are separate economic classes; R05's initially supported research/model lane must not treat them as model costs or admit their operations.

Loss/margin, supplier fulfilment, recurring commitments, browser/account changes and commerce remain unsupported unless a reviewed finite operation definition explicitly supports every necessary proof and limit. Unsupported controls block affected dispatch, rather than silently dropping the control. New operation definitions, purpose eligibility, financial compatibility evidence and server capabilities are private administrative evidence; migrations install no enabled credentials, capabilities or provider permissions.

## Exact identifiers and trusted preparation

Use the permanent Business ID as the immutable authority-root identity; maintain separate currency buckets under it. A new Quest, policy, workflow, experiment or day cannot reset this root or its lifetime exposure. A higher lifetime ceiling needs an explicit new structured owner confirmation, with expected current cap revision and a displayed total that includes all prior charges and unsettled exposure. A child controller cannot create this confirmation.

An administrator-provisioned, closed operation registry binds the exact operation key to pack/release/workflow, provider, purpose, data classes, category and maximum liability. Arbitrary R04 labels and model outputs never resolve by fuzzy matching. Qualification, source/provider application-purpose eligibility and rule-interpretation evidence have immutable hashes and explicit validity intervals. Accountless eligible public research uses a null account binding; unrelated account setup is not its prerequisite. A source domain alone is not eligibility evidence.

A trusted server prepares an immutable operation request from a confirmed policy, exact same-Business workflow, request hash, registered operation and fresh quote/proof. The request's cost cannot exceed any applicable policy/registry limit. Workers receive neither server credentials nor a grant-writing interface. Subsequent calls accept request ID and exact capability only; they reload the saved request rather than accepting replacement scope/caps from worker payloads.

Implemented public RPC surface (all narrowly granted, no private-table grants):

- `r05_policy_owner(business,operation,payload,submissionId)` for versioned policy proposals, exact confirmation, revocation and scoped pause/resume. Requires authenticated direct owner check and idempotent request hash. Scope targets are Business, Quest, installation or account; nonexistent/different-Business targets fail.
- `r05_admission_server(business,operation,payload,serverKey)` for trusted preparation, reservation, dispatch marker, unsent release, trusted settlement/readback and transport eligibility checks. The server authority is disabled unless separately provisioned. It never returns raw account credentials.
- `r05_admission_read(business,policy?,limit,offset)` for owner policy, currency-separated known/pending exposure, exact status and safe decision history. Invalid explicit identities fail without fallback.

The exact signatures use `p_business_id`, `p_operation`, `p_payload`, plus `p_submission_id` for `r05_policy_owner` and `p_server_key` for `r05_admission_server`. Read accepts `p_policy_id`, `p_limit`, `p_offset`. Types are in `src/core/admission-contract.ts`. Owner proposal payload is `OperatingPolicy`; confirmation/revocation accepts `{policyId,policyHash}` and pause/resume accepts `{kind,id}`. Confirmation compares both `expectedCapRevision` and `expectedExposureMicrounits` under the Business lock and refuses unresolved prior liability. The owner predicate is checked inside the mutation lock; transferred Businesses cannot retain the former owner's active policy/confirmation. Policy ceilings cannot exceed the exact R04 USD budget or deadline. Server responses distinguish `allowed`, `blocked` and `needs_owner`; denials produce immutable reason-code records without reflecting prompts or secrets.

`guard` accepts the trusted `AdmissionDispatchInput` at the final transport boundary. It binds the saved accounting request hash separately from SHA-256 of the owned wire body, exact provider model, actual UTF-8 body byte count and output-token ceiling. The administrative registry's quote must bound both input bytes and output tokens with its fixed maximum potential USD liability. No variable worker price estimate establishes authority. `prepare` saves the same exact request without dispatch; `reserve` and `dispatch` consume its request ID. Only the server authority can invoke these phases. Owner/session and worker capabilities alone have no grant-writing or settlement access.

## Reservation, dispatch, settlement and retries

Lock the Business first for policy confirmation, pause, reservation, dispatch and settlement, then referenced rows in deterministic order. Apply cumulative Business/root, policy, category and per-operation caps atomically. Reserve the full potential liability before dispatch. Recheck policy/rules/Goal revisions, time, stop state, caps, dispatch cadence/count, qualification, eligibility, source freshness and account revision immediately before writing the immutable dispatch marker.

The marker is the linearization point: pause that wins the Business lock first prevents dispatch; a marker that wins first is already committed in-flight work. Record the marker before any network call, and return `shouldDispatch=false` on every replay. A marker is never cleared or moved to another request. An adapter crash between marker and network leaves uncertain liability, never automatic retry.

Unsent release is allowed only when no marker exists, and its release event is immutable. Once marked, reservation/unknown exposure remains even after failure, cancellation, pause, revocation or expiry. Trusted settlement accepts exact provider request ID, currency, amount and receipt hash; known actual charges above a cap are recorded truthfully and block further admission. Missing amount is unknown, never zero. A provider receipt cannot settle two operations.

New-ledger `settle`/`readback` takes `{requestId,currency,actualMicrounits,providerRequestId,receiptHash}` and appends immutable evidence. Unknown-to-known reconciliation never replaces earlier rows; exposure uses the maximum known actual amount. Repeated receipt identity cannot settle another operation. No RPC erases a marker or authorizes retry of a marked request. Owner consent cannot clear uncertain provider charges or technical missing evidence. Automatic effect retries are unsupported; a new operation needs its own exact trusted request and all normal bounds.

Commerce readback is a separate `existing_effect_read` server operation with `{provider,runId,requestHash,sentAt,connectionId,connectionRevision,endpoint}`. It requires an existing Printful product operation/Etsy publication activation row, exact saved run and marker, current account revision/owner, and separately provisioned per-run `existing_effect_readback` application-purpose/data-use evidence bound to the current Business owner. SQL derives the finite allowed GET endpoints from the saved source/package/state; transport also verifies GET and the exact final endpoint. It returns `shouldRead`, always `shouldDispatch=false`, and cannot mutate markers, financial settlement or provider effects. Business pause and expired financial consent do not prevent these qualified reads of existing effects. Missing eligibility or current account access does. No readback evidence is seeded and Etsy holds/Printful credential custody remain independent gates.

## Existing accounting and dispatch compatibility

Existing research liability remains authoritative in `product_research_cost_reservations` / `product_research_cost_settlements`, including every root, round, `budgetAuthorityRootId`, `semanticGoalHash` and funding approval. R05 reads these rows rather than copying/resetting them. Creative, listing and listing-qualification cost ledgers plus model-invocation telemetry need equivalent accounting adapters. Known actual cost is charged; unresolved cost retains at least the reservation/estimate and blocks fresh effects. Never count one mirrored call in both a legacy ledger and a new ledger: an accounting adapter must name an exact immutable source identity or use only the new ledger. Unknown/unmapped legacy exposure blocks admission until reconciled.

Legacy money reservations serialize with the same Business lock and cap checks once an R05 policy governs that Business, through additive triggers on the original reservation/settlement tables. Existing function bodies and grants remain unchanged. Known settlements above a cap are recorded truthfully. Sidecar markers can bind an exact fresh legacy reservation without changing its receipt/input; any prior settlement, including an unknown receipt, prevents claiming that source as a fresh attempt. A source must postdate its registered operation validity start. Preparation alone never clears unknown exposure. A trusted `release_unsent` request requires an evidence hash, exact source binding and absence of any marker/effect receipt. Owners and workers cannot invoke it.

Read-through covers research, creative, listing, listing qualification and model-invocation USD telemetry. Mirrored listing telemetry is excluded only when exact immutable task, worker, workflow, Business, provider/model, receipt and amount match the authoritative listing source. Receipt equality alone never merges different calls: historical collisions retain both liabilities and block fresh admission. A global atomic provider-receipt claim covers all new authoritative legacy and R05 settlements, including cross-Business races. Unknown unidentified reservations retain exposure. New sidecar reservations are excluded from new-ledger sums when their original ledger remains authoritative. Model-invocation accounting can be observed but cannot be nominated for new dispatch; unadapted raw model paths remain closed. There is no automatic assignment of an unbound legacy workflow to a Quest. A workflow must carry the exact managed Goal or a validated R04 research association.

`legacy_settle` takes `{kind,runId,workflowRunId,runtimeCapability,callKey,reportedMicrousd,providerRequestId,receipt}` under the server key. It validates exact saved source and scoped capability, claims the provider receipt, appends trusted attestation, and invokes the original guarded settlement RPC in one transaction; failures roll everything back. Research uses `runId:null` and `receipt:{}`. Creative/listing/qualification retain their original receipt payloads. Governed legacy settlement writes without exact trusted attestation are denied, so a worker capability cannot forge zero cost to free budget. For an already immutable legacy receipt, a research amount above its legacy int32 storage range, or an expired creative capability, trusted late readback appends financial evidence without rewriting the old receipt or resuming its workflow. Maximum actual evidence is retained. Initial settlements return the original RPC result; late readback returns `{recorded:true,r05Readback:true}`.

Definite denied dispatch preparation retains an exact request ID even when no policy is confirmed. The trusted runtime may call `release_unsent` after that explicit denied response; timeout/lost response/ambiguous result never authorizes release. SQL independently requires no marker and no legacy settlement. Original reservation/history and old root guards remain conservative; R05 releases only the proven-unsent source in its own exposure projection and does not manufacture a zero-cost provider receipt or retry an old attempt.

R05 cannot be called complete from an isolated new ledger. Owner-facing Pause Business must remain explicitly limited until every affected transport is integrated and tested. Settlements and readback remain available after pause/expiry; they may record already-incurred liability but cannot launch an unapproved replacement effect.

`r05_admission_read` is a STABLE, nonlocking owner snapshot and works in a read-only transaction. It exposes only safe operation choices and proposal hashes, currency-separated held/unknown totals, current cap revisions, scoped pause preferences and decision codes. Policies/decisions are paginated to 50 with exact `policyTotal`/`decisionTotal`; pause states are limited to 50 with `pauseStatesComplete`. Explicit NULL pagination is rejected, and serialized results above 262144 bytes fail with `r05_read_too_large_reduce_page` rather than returning an unbounded payload. `serverAuthorityConfigured` is configuration information, never a claim that the selected Business/Quest can execute. The first shipped registry admits only fixed USD model operations; commerce, supplier/refund/sales/fee effects, FX, recurring commitments, loss/margin controls and automatic target-based stopping are explicitly unsupported. Their transports fail closed. Administrative qualification, rule interpretation and provider-purpose evidence are missing live prerequisites, not authority inferred from an old confirmation.

Use actual SQL owner/capability isolation tests and real multi-session lock-wait proofs for concurrent reservations, split-operation cap evasion, same submission replay, policy edit/revoke, pause-versus-marker and settlement. Cover currencies/precision, separate categories, repeated Quests, old research chains, unknown/over-cap actual charges, provider receipt reuse, stale proof/account revisions and no automatic retry after markers.
