# Goal-driven public research: smallest useful slice

Status: focused implementation; isolated mechanics pass, live acceptance pending. This document creates no operational authority.
Baseline: PR 90 merge `fe548d4959ece71a7d9af9b67ed10079769ba6e1`, tree
`c6ed4a9c13774add7e6f4d7af913ff53bd0a4fdd`.

## Outcome

An owner enters a general research goal and a bounded budget. The application
plans candidate hypotheses and a relevant public search, obtains actual source
excerpts through its existing search provider, selects evidence, compares the
hypotheses, and independently reviews a sourced recommendation or an honest
insufficient-evidence result. The first live acceptance goal is “Research viable print-on-demand products to sell on Etsy”. No candidate, source result, winner, or demand
claim may be supplied by a fixture, implementation code, or an outside assistant.

## Reuse and scope

Reuse the existing five phases: plan → search1 → select1 → strategy → review,
R07 scheduling, R05 atomic reservations/dispatch, exact generation receipts,
source/evidence hashes, independent review, Stop and reconciliation. This is one
bounded research episode, not an unrestricted agent loop. The app discovers
product types, niches and audiences; neither country, season, theme, product
shortlist nor final candidate count is an owner requirement. Internal working
set limits are finite implementation bounds, not a preselected outcome. Keep the existing
five-call/one-collection topology and current independently priced bounds for the
first useful slice. Nothing here permits product execution or shop changes.

Introduce a separately versioned POD research-goal scope and result. Historical
POD/owner-capture/Steel contracts remain unchanged. Do not adapt the Insights-only
source receipt or its hardcoded default qualification. Do not manufacture a new
budget root, map old grants into this scope, or renew stopped windows.

## Owner journey

1. Select the existing Business and save the research goal and USD budget.
2. Review one plain-language packet: the public brief being shared, permitted
   source/query policy, denied sources, recipients/retention, maximum calls/cost,
   cumulative existing exposure, deadline, Stop and possible insufficiency.
3. Confirm that exact packet. The application generates internal IDs and derives
   the finite execution authorization; UUID/operator enrollment is not a normal
   owner step. Creating or editing intent grants no authority.
4. Observe progress and open the resulting cited report. Reopening the stable
   run reconciles it; a lost response cannot create another run. Stop prevents
   further dispatch while preserving already-sent receipts and liabilities.

## Query, source and data boundary

The approved public brief is the only owner text available to the planner and
search. No account tokens, browser state, customer/order data, unrelated Business
context or private source can enter this path. Credential detection complements
explicit public-data disclosure; it does not certify arbitrary text as safe.

The planner returns actual bounded query text, source choices/rationale and
unproven hypotheses. Accepted plan output, not an owner-supplied query or model
assertion of permission, determines the exact later search descriptor. Trusted
code/SQL validates it against the confirmed policy and persists actual query,
domains, wire, source and recipient provenance. Queries inside policy do not
need new owner enrollment; changed policy, recipients, data categories or cost
bounds do.

Source acquisition uses the existing documented search API and factual excerpts,
not direct arbitrary page scraping. The source eligibility policy requires
independent review before implementation activation. It must support genuinely
relevant dynamic sources and cannot be a tiny gift-survey allowlist. Mandatory
Etsy marketplace exclusions, private/internal hosts, authentication paths,
credential-bearing URLs, known prohibited uses and unsupported source rights
remain blocked. API availability is not a publisher licence or Etsy demand data.

All retrieved text is untrusted evidence. It cannot change prompts, permission,
recipients, budgets, tools or schemas. Source references must resolve to actual
retained excerpts. Review must distinguish observations, inference, contrary
findings, absent sales/cost evidence and unknowns. No numerical ranking is a
probability of commercial success.

## Approval and accounting boundary

The additive owner-enrollment/security migration needs independent review and
exact production approval. Its installation seeds no owner grant or paid run.
A live episode requires a fresh quote and exact owner approval. The fresh permission uses cumulative Business R05 accounting. No old research
root, stopped envelope, units or expiry is mapped into it. Existing costs remain counted. The original USD 0.025 unknown
hold is not erased, assumed settled, transferred or replaced. Supported exact
billing reconciliation is inspected separately before a live-run proposal.

## Focused acceptance and negative cases

- Two different general goals produce goal-specific planner/search requests;
  no fixed niche, query, audience, country or winner is embedded in the code.
- Actual accepted planner query/source choices reach the real search wire;
  legacy modes keep their original fixed-query behavior.
- Dynamic choices within the reviewed policy do not re-enroll permission;
  denied domains, private inputs and out-of-policy choices fail before send.
- Source instructions cannot expand access; invented citations or substituted
  source receipts cannot reach an accepted recommendation.
- Owner/Business mismatch, stale edits, revoked/expired approval, exhausted cap,
  unknown liability and concurrent duplicate activation fail closed.
- One immutable approval and request identity survives interrupted confirmation,
  compatible deployment and receipt recovery without duplicate paid dispatch.
- Stop blocks new sends and preserves pending receipts and unknown costs.
- Isolated tests prove mechanics only. The final useful milestone is an actual
  app-generated, source-grounded result for the acceptance goal, with real call,
  cost and source provenance and no hand-fed research.

## Implemented boundary

- Separate immutable POD worker/workflow releases reuse the five-phase engine;
  old geographic charters are not relabeled or silently qualified.
- One reviewed application template pins source semantics, schemas and eligible
  worker releases. It is installed once per reviewed contract, not per owner
  query or software deployment. Installation creates no owner grant.
- The owner-only prepare/confirm path derives IDs and role-separated keys,
  obtains a fresh existing quote and genuine R05 confirmation, and creates a
  finite 30-minute dispatch window plus 30-minute receipt window.
- The source sidecar explicitly declares deferred domains. R05 admission and
  wire checks resolve actual domains from the accepted planner receipt. Empty
  domains cannot become a general permission; legacy equality stays unchanged.
- Workflow runs the same R07 controller independently of an owner browser tab.
  Interrupted sends are not replayed. Delayed metadata uses the saved finite
  receipt attempts; Stop permits exact paid reconciliation and unsent cancellation.
- Binding stores preparation release; the atomic first-send claim records the
  actual executing release, so an interrupted compatible redeploy remains honest.
- A research-only R04 intent needs no invented commercial target or geography.
  Other R10/R11/direct Steel execution lanes explicitly reject that discriminator.

The source policy uses search-API factual snippets, not an assertion that all
publishers license analytics. Domain filters cannot prove which upstream paths
Exa retrieved. Returned auth/private paths and credential-like URLs are rejected;
retained source content remains untrusted evidence. The approval discloses Exa
query retention/improvement use separately from inference ZDR controls.

## Evidence so far

The focused PGlite suite exercises genuine owner R04/R05/R07 APIs, the real
five-phase builders and OpenRouter serializer with inert responses and route
receipts. It covers wrong owner/capability/Business, deferred-domain substitution,
unknown marked liability, cancellation of exact unsent reservations, pending
receipt settlement after Stop plus template revocation, and immutable results.
Separate adapter tests cover old-bind/new-deployment first-send provenance,
duplicate prevention and two delayed metadata lookups followed by success.
These tests are engineering evidence only. No new live provider, grant, template,
cap or migration has been activated. The original held USD 0.025 is unresolved.

## Release and live-run boundary

Before Production: complete normal exact-head checks and independent review;
request exact approval for the definition-only migration and reviewed application
catalog/template bundle. Preserve the old stopped setup and its immutable history.
The migration installs no credentials, template rows, owner approval or paid run.
Then obtain a fresh finite owner packet for the general acceptance goal. Any
supported reconciliation of the old USD 0.025 needs its own exact-operation
billing evidence and authorization; it is not implied by the new permission.
The useful acceptance is the resulting real app-produced report and receipts,
not fixtures or a separately authored research report.
