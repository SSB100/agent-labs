# ADR 003: Etsy-first evidence and decision baselines

Status: accepted direction on 10 October 2026; implementation and qualification pending. This does not describe deployed capability or close R12.

## Problem and decision

The current owner research path discards the planner's proposed query focus and collects a fixed profile of broad retail statistics. Those sources cannot establish demand for an Etsy T-shirt hypothesis. Repeating that collection with more model calls cannot resolve the missing channel evidence.

Start with actual Etsy Marketplace Insights observations for candidate demand and actual shop performance for subsequent experiments. Use supplier/account facts for feasibility and deterministic pricing; use permitted public retrieval for a named remaining question. Preserve the canonical geographic Goal and its four-market objective. Etsy account locale is not buyer geography, and an unsegmented observation cannot decide that comparison.

Use generic, immutable, attributed owner observations through the normal owner interface. Each selected record needs its exact source/interface, capture time, reporting window, units/currency, source text/content hash, coverage and limitations. Owner attestation establishes provenance, not independent verification by Etsy. Pin the selected records and disclosure into preparation, approval, execution and citations. Never relabel owner observations as Exa collections or resurrect the exhausted addendum lane.

## Baseline rules

Before selecting a winner, record two or three comparable hypotheses and a negative/reference comparison using the same product format, reporting window and currency display. Retain missing and discouraging observations. Exploratory additions create an explicitly revised comparison; they do not erase the old baseline.

Record displayed searches, result counts, relative conversion labels and purchase-price ranges with their definitions. Rounded values stay rounded. Result counts are not unique competitors or item sales. Conversion labels remain ordinal; do not invent percentages or assign numerical distances between bands. Marketplace prices do not establish contribution margin.

A research decision identifies a falsifiable hypothesis, a useful next experiment and success, failure and inconclusive criteria. The independent review rubric assesses reasoning quality; its score is not measured demand or probability of commercial success. Weak evidence can justify a targeted follow-up or pivot, rather than ending the Goal or forcing a positive result.

Later performance decisions need a declared eligible-exposure denominator, observation duration, attribution window and comparison. Zero visits means no exposure and inconclusive conversion evidence. There is no justified universal visits/orders threshold in the present evidence; the actual experiment must establish its own rule before its result is assessed.

Calculate costs, contribution margin and break-even with the existing deterministic pricing code and verified inputs. Unknown costs leave the result unknown. Settled profit requires actual fees, supplier charges, refunds and currency treatment, with pending obligations tracked separately.

## Access and execution boundaries

The official Etsy OpenAPI specification inspected on 10 October has no documented Marketplace Insights keyword metrics or shop traffic/conversion operation. Existing shop/listing read access is not those capabilities. The ordinary signed-in owner interface is a genuine available source; no automated Insights API or scraping permission is assumed.

Five research responsibilities need not always require five paid model calls. A future explicit version may use genuine owner collection and deterministic validation with paid planner, strategist and independent reviewer roles. It must record its actual operations and liabilities. The existing five-phase format and historical receipts remain unchanged; skipping its phases is not an implementation shortcut.

The USD 10 total run ceiling and ten combined additional actions remain in force. Source changes, repairs and pivots cannot replenish money, reset Goal counters or change funding roots. A capability gap pauses for a genuine source operation; it does not manufacture evidence. Artwork, listings, advertising, orders and sales remain outside the current research authorization.

## Acceptance

- Normal owner save, review, selection, exact preflight, confirmation and citable execution; no privileged manual enrollment or sample-specific path.
- Cross-Business, changed-content, forged-span, stale approval and unapproved-disclosure rejection; captured text cannot grant authority.
- Negative and held-out observations can produce honest TEST, NEEDS_MORE_EVIDENCE or REJECT; no guaranteed positive outcome.
- Genuine paid-call counts, costs, Stop and late receipt behavior; preserved historical records, root liabilities and lifetime counters.
- Independent complete-diff review, required exact-tree gates and actual owner browser verification before live qualification.
